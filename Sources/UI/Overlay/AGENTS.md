# UI/Overlay module

Module `UIOverlay` in `.agents/modules.json`. Parent: `Sources/UI/AGENTS.md`.

## Owns

Everything on screen while you dictate or record, plus the dictation session itself:

- **The Notch island** (`NotchIsland*`), the only dictation, meeting and call-prompt window. One black shape grows out of the notch (or hangs from the top edge of a display without one) and carries dictation, the live meeting, the call-detected Record / Not now / Later prompt, and "Who spoke?" (the speaker review). The old dictation panel, meeting pill, call prompt pill and speaker naming window are deleted.
- **Three controllers that feed it:** `FloatingOverlayController` (dictation states, timers, global Esc monitor, not-pasted notice), `MeetingOverlayController` (recording state, warnings, prompts, Open, right-click "Discard Recording…") and `CapturePillController` (detected-meeting prompt and its timeout). Each keeps its state machine, timers and actions, has no window, and pushes a plain `NotchIsland*Content` snapshot; the island routes taps back. Detected-meeting Record / Not now / Remind live only in `CapturePillController`.
- **`DictationSessionController`** and its `+*.swift` extensions: start, stop, paste-back, persistence, recovery, presses, the session cap (`TranscriptedConstants.dictationSessionMaxDuration`, two hours in the fork), telemetry. Speech-side control flow (recovery wait loop, model-warmup wait, STTRouter decisions) lives in `Sources/Speech/DictationSession.swift`; this folder keeps panel geometry, tooltips, accessibility labels, paste-back, persistence and telemetry.
- Dictation and meeting presentation policies. The start/stop policies and `DictationTrigger` live in Speech.

## Public surface

`DictationSessionController`, `FloatingOverlayController`, `MeetingOverlayController`, `CapturePillController`, `NotchIslandController`, `NotchIslandSpeakerReviewView`, `NotchIslandSpeakerReviewContent`, `NotchIslandSpeakerReviewPolicy`, `MeetingDurationFormatter`. AppShell builds the controllers; Capture routes presses into `DictationSessionController`; Settings asks the island for speaker review; the menu bar formats the meeting timer.

## May depend on

UIShared, AppState, Meeting, Dictation, Speech, Support, Observability, and Core's `core-vocab` tier. Only AppShell, Capture, UIMenuBar and UISettings may depend on UIOverlay. `.agents/modules.json` is the source of truth.


- Dictation owns `DictationSessionCapWarningPolicy`; the overlay consumes its countdown and accessible announcement.

## Where to start

Island:

- `NotchIslandPresentation.swift` is Foundation-pure and decides what the wings and drop-down show for every dictation, meeting and call-prompt state. The newest thing going on has the island (fork): a dictation over a live meeting takes both wings and the meeting becomes a layer behind it (`backLayers`, see the dictation queue below); the call prompt waits while a dictation runs; with a drop-down open the wings only report status. Change behavior here, not in the view.
- `NotchIslandGeometry.swift` is pure geometry (notch detection, content-sized wings, the 460 pt drop-down, envelope, screen-width clamp) plus `NotchIslandMotion` (springs, fades, blur timing).
- `NotchIslandController.swift` keeps the island's state once and draws it on every display (fork): one `NotchIslandSurface` (panel, view, display, target frame, shape spring) per display, built at launch (`prewarm`). Any change in the displays (`NotchIslandDisplays`: one plugged in or out, moved, resized, the notch coming or going) drops every window and draws the island again on fresh ones without the grow; the live transcript and dictation preview have one view per surface. Hover on any display opens the drop-down on all of them. Desktops add no copies: each display's one panel joins every desktop. A surface no one can see (`NotchIslandSurface.isCovered`: its display asleep or locked, or the window covered, as the window server reports it) gets no level bars, dictation bars or review picture until it is seen again. "Who spoke?" is one live view, so it sits on one surface (the display under the pointer when it opened) and the others show a picture of it (`NotchIslandSpeakerReviewMirror`, `cacheDisplay` every 0.1 s while it is up on more than one display); the pointer reaching the island on another display moves the live view there, swapped in place in the drop-down's stack (`NotchIslandDropSwap`), and a name being typed moves with it and keeps the keyboard, as it does through a replug (`NotchIslandController+SpeakerReview.swift`). Each panel joins every desktop; macOS can still leave one on a single desktop after a replug, so `NotchIslandController+Spaces.swift` checks on show, desktop switch and display change and `NotchIslandPanel.rejoinAllSpacesIfLeftBehind()` drops `canJoinAllSpaces` and sets it back a main-queue turn apart (again 0.7 s after a rebuild) (a hit logs `overlay.island_rejoined_all_desktops` to the local events.jsonl). Motion is Core Animation: fixed-size envelope, a mask layer that springs out of the notch, nothing resizes mid-animation. Mouse monitors make the envelope click-through except over the island and drive hover (drop-down opens 0.12 s in, 0.38 s out; messages and prompts open it themselves; a finished dictation lingers about 2.6 s with Copy / Paste again).
- `NotchIslandView.swift` is AppKit drawing (no SwiftUI hosting), with `NotchIslandButton`, `NotchIslandBarsView`, `NotchIslandSmallViews` (ring, dots, shimmer), `NotchIslandLayerStackView` (fork: the layers behind the island), `NotchIslandView+Blur` (show-time blur set up once during the alpha-0 prewarm). `NotchIslandPanel.swift` is the borderless non-activating status-level panel.
- Dictation waveform: `NotchIslandLevelScroller` steps bars at `audioMeteringInterval` (20/s) on the display clock, at most once per frame, no catch-up burst. `NotchIslandController+DictationBars.swift` feeds it and runs `NotchIslandFrameClock` (an `NSView.displayLink`) only while a take listens on screen.
- Meeting wing levels: one row (`NotchIslandMeetingLevelRow`, drawn by `NotchIslandMeetingLevelsView` in `NotchIslandBarsView.swift`). Your bars enter on the right and move left, the call's enter on the left and move right, and they cross; at rest the whole row is dim accent dots and only your audible bars go full accent. Core publishes levels only every 0.15 s, so readings just pick the next bar and the view's own `Timer` steps the row every 0.11 s (no display link). The timer stops once the row is at rest with no readings, because a hidden island keeps its last wing items. Dictating mid-meeting gives the island to the take; the meeting shows as the deepest layer behind it (a red dot and small level bars).
- Dictation hover: `NotchIslandDictationPreviewView`, `NotchIslandDropView+Dictation`, `NotchIslandController+DictationPreview` show a four-line window onto the whole take from `LiveDictationCaptions` (follows the newest words unless you scroll up; last two words dimmed), then Cancel and "Insert into <app>". The words stay through Writing and Pasted and crossfade into the written text, which the recent-insert hover then keeps. The controller sets `NotchIslandDictationContent.showsLivePreview`. To keep hover cheap, `NotchIslandView` keeps the drop-down built between hovers while the take is spoken (`NotchIslandDrop.staysBuiltBetweenHovers`, `NotchIslandController+DictationDrop.swift`), the app icon is drawn once per take (`NotchIslandAppIconCache`), and the drop-down height is measured once per render. With more than one thing going on (a take and a meeting, or takes finishing behind the island) the hover is a list of cards instead (`.activities`, below).
- Meeting drop-down: `NotchIslandLiveTranscriptView` + `NotchIslandController+LiveTranscript.swift` show the scrolling live transcript fed from `LiveMeetingCaptions` (one view kept across rebuilds; appends finished words, replaces only the faded tail). Copy all (`meetingCopyTranscript`) is handled by the island.
- Speaker review ("Who spoke?", approved design: voice prints): `NotchIslandSpeakerReviewPolicy` (Foundation-pure rules, including each row's title, line under it, buttons, lit rings, celebration and footer count), `NotchIslandSpeakerReviewView` (+`+Footer`: colors, clip-less recognized names, the footer's glowing dots, the print tip), `NotchIslandVoiceRowView` (+`+Layout`, +`+Naming`), `NotchIslandSpeakerReviewControls` (title-as-name-field, chips, suggestions, Not a person ×) and `NotchIslandVoicePrintControls` (print slot, round ✕ / ✓, text buttons, dots, tip). It is the only post-meeting review. Each row's `VoicePrintView` is the person's face and their clip's play button (one ring per confirmed meeting, scaled to a lineup's lower bar; colors from `VoicePrintStyle.colorIndices` in row order, and sticky for the review so answering one row never recolors another). Asked rows read "Marcus Reed?" with ✕ and ✓; ✓ and naming a new voice in place play the match animation (`celebrate`), Undo cancels it; the line under the name is `SpeakerNamingTierPresentation.reviewHint`. ✕, or a voice with no guess, makes the title itself the name field; with the keyboard the first three calendar invitees fade in under it as one-tap names; autocomplete from saved people, invitees first; the 20 s Later ring runs only while the review is on screen and not hovered; Return/Tab and Done/Later save the arrowed-to row, else an exact match, else the typed name as a new person, never a longer saved name. A calendar 1:1 prefills the remote voice's name. "All me" keeps local mic voices as You; "Not a person" discards (Keep as You wins over discard). The footer shows glowing dots and "N people named automatically"; a dot whose print a ✓ just completed lands after the print settles, one beat at a time: dot, then sparkle, then the count ticking up (`VoicePrintCascadePlan.FooterLanding`). Undo after ✕ asks again (`undoTarget`); moving on after an answer takes the keyboard only for a row whose title is the name field (`takesKeyboardOnAdvance`). Leave `VoicePrintView.cascadeOutset` unclipped around prints (rows have no card and nothing clips; the island's own shape still trims the full burst at its edge). It builds the `SpeakerNameUpdate`s Core writes back and reports analytics with `surface: speaker_review_island`. `meeting_speaker_match_reviewed` carries `auto_recognized`, so a correction of a silently named voice counts as a wrong silent name. Voices named silently show only the name; hovering offers "Not <name>?", and Undo keeps them. When every remote voice was recognized it lists who was on the call, asks nothing; Core queues that review only while `reviewListsRecognizedVoicesProvider` says the island is selected, and it closes itself after about two minutes even while hidden (`NotchIslandSpeakerReviewPolicy.recognizedOnlyHardCapSeconds`).
- `AnchorMarkerController` (fork) draws the Anchored paste markers, one per take (`AnchorMarkerView.swift` holds the SwiftUI flag and its layout): each a small black capsule in the island's look, with no ghost caret (caret bounds are often wrong): while listening, seven accent level dots fed by the dictation meter (center-weighted, dots at rest), the take's timer (from when listening began, like the island's) and a stop button; then the dots' writing ripple, a white come-back arrow while the paste waits, an accent check. It sits 3 pt above the field's box, left edges lined up (`AnchorMarkerPlacement`): the box is what the user sees as the text box, found by `AnchoredTextDelivery.fieldBox` (`AnchoredPastePolicy.fieldBoxDepth`; T3 Code's composer card sits 16 pt outside its text area). A box right under the title bar takes it below; a box filling the window (a terminal, an editor) inside its top-right corner. The box is read off the main thread about four times a second (one read at a time per flag, dropped once the flag is gone) and the panel flies to a new spot in 0.25 s through `animator()`, since a composer grows when it takes focus; between reads, and while the window is dragged, it jumps with the window (a zero-length `animator()` frame, which also stops a flight; a plain `setFrame` would not). The stop button calls `stopFromAnchorMarker` (the island's stop path); the panel takes the mouse only while the pointer is over that button (`ignoresMouseEvents` flipped each tick, `AnchorMarkerLayout.stopButtonRect`), accepts the first click and never becomes key, so the anchored app keeps focus and every other click reaches it. The panel sits one step above that window in the stacking order (re-pinned at 15 Hz the way cua-driver pins its agent cursor) and hides while the window is off screen; its animation clock and box reads stop then, and Reduce Motion stops the clock and the flight. It is not part of the island and never takes focus; the island keeps showing the take. Its `sharingType` follows `NotchIslandPreferences.visibleInScreenSharing`.
- Several takes, several flags, keyed by the take's session id. A take keeps its flag through the hand-off and updates it from its own stop task; a take that ends without its words landing drops it (`takeTaskEnded`, `pasteAnchorSessionEnded`, which skips a handed-off take), a deferred paste keeps it waiting (`DeferredAnchoredPaste.markerID`). Flags over one box (its left edge and bottom within 24 pt: a composer may grow between two flags' reads) sit side by side (`AnchorMarkerPlacement.row`): the newest take's full flag first, then the older takes' compact ones (dots or glyph only) in start order.
- In T3 Code, Codex and Claude (`ChatThreadPolicy.sidebarBundleIDs`) a flag keeps to its take's chat thread (`follow`, from `beginChatThreadCapture`): over the box only while the window shows that thread; with another thread open it flies (0.4 s), compact, to the bottom-right corner of the thread's sidebar row, left of what the app draws there (T3 Code's agent badge, Codex's unread dot or status, Claude's status and options button: `ChatSidebar.rowBadgeWidth`, `AnchorMarkerPlacement.rowCorner`), and hides while that row can't be seen (sidebar hidden or slid off, row scrolled away or not rendered), coming back with it. One read per window about four times a second gives the open thread (`ChatThreadReader.pagesNow`) and the rows (`Sources/Accessibility/ChatSidebarReader.swift`; every fourth read while every flag there shows its thread and knows its row). The row is the one marked current while the thread was open (in Claude's Code sessions, which nothing marks, the one row named as the open page: `ChatThreadPolicy.openThreadName`), learned only from a read whose open thread was the same before and after the rows (a switch in between would teach it the next thread's row), and found again by its title and project (`ChatThreadPolicy.sidebarRow`). An unreadable page keeps the flag where it was, and the last rows stand for two reads that found none.
- `CapturePillController` owns the call-prompt countdown. `CallPromptTimeoutClock` pauses while the pointer is over the island and while the prompt waits behind a dictation (off-screen waiting capped at two minutes, then it expires unanswered).
- `MeetingPromptPriority` is the one precedence rule for the overlay's four warning prompts (audio inactivity, system-audio degradation, route instability, mic boost). `MeetingDurationFormatter` is timer formatting.

Dictation session (`DictationSessionController` plus):

- `+RecordingStart` (opening the mic, warmup, recovery wait, permission errors), `+Stop` (checkpoint, transcribe, paste or save), `+PasteBack` (not-pasted notices, the paste, Auto Enter), `+Persistence`, `+Recovery` (saved-audio cleanup, Quit admission, Retry Saving, interruptions), `+RecordingSpool` (fork: the take on disk while it records; begun by `startDictation`, ended by Stop before its checkpoint, by a discard, a quit or an interruption), `+Presses` (queued starts, modifier combos, early release, a tap keeping the take listening), `+HandOff` (fork: the dictation queue, below), `+AnchoredPaste` (fork: Anchored paste; captures the field at start, routes the paste at stop, holds deferred pastes until the user is back, presses send for a background delivery; see `Sources/Accessibility/AGENTS.md`; `autoSendDecision(for:bundleID:)` is the one place a take's send decision is made, per-app rules, then Anchored Enter, then `DictationAutoSendPolicy.applyingMode`), `+SendConfirmation` (fork: `DictationAutoSendMode.confirmAfterPaste`; every take whose words landed in its anchored box joins `DictationSendConfirmationQueue` (`Sources/Dictation/`) in take order and is asked in turn, one prompt at a time: "Pasted. Press ↩ to send it in X." with Send on the island, which with more waiting quotes the take's first words and says how many wait; the shown prompt arms the event tap's confirm key through `sendConfirmationArm` for its own full wait. The take on the island, or a deferred paste landing while no take runs, asks in place of what the island shows for it; a handed-off take waits until no take is on the island and nothing else is up (`showNextSendConfirmationWhenIslandIsFree`, every 0.5 s); a save failure shows its error first and asks after. A regular paste is asked about only if it went into the anchored box. A confirm presses send where that take's words went, read at that moment by `DictationAutoSendPolicy.confirmedSend`; in the background only while the box still holds the take's words (`boxChanged` otherwise). Dismiss, Esc or the wait running out drop the shown one and send nothing; anything else taking the island (a take starting, another message) puts it back first in line (`onActionableMessageDiscarded`'s `dismissed` tells them apart), and its prompt gives way to a waiting press. A send whose box read the words back when they landed and reads empty by its turn (sent or cleared by hand) is passed over.), `+ChatThreadGuard` (fork: reads the AI chat thread at start; the regular paste and every Enter go only while that app still shows it, else "Not pasted" with the words on the clipboard, or a notice that Enter wasn't pressed), `+ChatThreadSend` (fork: a take that sends goes to its T3 Code or Codex thread while the user looks elsewhere, through T3 Code's server or into the Codex thread's queue, at once or after the island's confirm; the usual way when the app can't take it), `+SessionCap`, `+Muffle` (mute other audio while dictating; rules in `DictationMufflePolicy`), `+Telemetry`, and `DictationSessionDeliveryTypes`.
- `DictationStartAdmission` decides whether a press becomes a take and counts it. A press while dictating or queued is not counted yet; every other press is counted (`dictation_start_requested`) before any guard can refuse it, and each refusal reports its own reason. `DictationSessionPipeline` holds the start/stop wiring behind `DictationSessionPipelineHost` (the controller conforms): refusal messages, Try Again marked as retries, the start click (once per session, queued before the fast-path mic start), stop from the stale-task fence through checkpoint and model wait, empty takes (mis-tap, no speech, Paste Anyway, saved recording, Retry Saving) and Quit. Tests run both on fakes.
- Policies: `DictationEscapeCancelPolicy` (a short take cancels on the first Esc; a long one asks "Press Esc again to discard" and a second press within 3 s discards; fork: once the stop is admitted, `escapeKeepsTake` skips the confirm and Esc only declines the paste, see `DictationSessionController+TextRefinement.swift`), `DictationQueuedStartPolicy` (a press while the last take is finishing is remembered, from the stop's admission on, and waits up to 15 s instead of being refused; with the dictation queue it waits only for the stop checkpoint and model, see below), `DictationStartCuePolicy` (the start click plays on key press for a built-in or wired mic, but waits until recording starts on a headset, any input that could be one, or a mic `PinnedDictationSpeedPath` moved back to the engine), `DictationSessionCapWarningPolicy` (live "28s left" in the last 30 s before the session cap, worded for push-to-talk vs a hands-free take), `DictationWarmupPresentationPolicy` (model-warmup copy, before vs after the stop; `DictationPostStopModelWaitPolicy` keeps paste-back on the original app after a long wait), `DictationNoSpeechPresentationPolicy` (no-speech copy; "Transcribe It" runs the same import as Capture → Transcribe Audio File), `DictationMicrophoneLoadingPresentationPolicy`, `DictationMeterPolicy` (when the meter renders, clamps level), `DictationOverlayPlacementPolicy` (AX rect to Cocoa; no caller in the fork since the island draws on every display), `DictationStartActivation` (optional foreground-activation retry after a failed background mic start; a recovery attempt, not a readiness signal).

Dictation queue (fork, `DictationSessionController+HandOff.swift`):

- A start press while the last take is finishing doesn't wait for its transcription. Once that take's audio is safe in its WAV and the model is ready, it takes its audio out of the engine (`STTRouter.detachStoppedDictation`, `Sources/Speech/STTRouter+DetachedDictation.swift`: samples, the model's foreground use, Breeze's live pieces) and becomes hand-off-ready, so a waiting press hands it off and the next take records at once while this one is transcribed. Detached takes are transcribed one at a time in stop order (`DictationTranscriptionLine`) and carry their own empty reason and held-back text (`DictationFinishingTake.detachedTranscript`). A take that couldn't detach (no snapshot, a stale claim) holds the engine as before and hands off after its transcription. During the stop checkpoint, the model wait or the paste itself the press waits; the writing wing shows a mic meanwhile (`nextTakeWaiting`). Start gates read `STTRouter.blocksNewDictationStart` (the engine holds a take's buffer, or Transcribe again runs), not `isTranscribing`, which detached takes keep true.
- The stop task runs inside `DictationTakeScope.$take`. After the hand-off the controller's per-take properties (session id, start time, source app, paste target, trigger, stopped audio, refine task, anchor, Auto Enter decision, chat thread) read and write the take's own copy from inside that task, so the stop code is unchanged and the next take starts clean. `activeTakeIsLive` and `endActiveTake()` stand in for `isDictating` in the stop path; a handed-off take never touches the island or the runtime diagnostics (it does update its own anchor marker), and a notice for a take that didn't paste waits until the island is free.
- Pastes keep take order (`DictationDeliveryOrder`, in `Sources/Dictation/`): a later, shorter take waits for earlier ones to paste and press send (fork: the turn passes after Auto Enter, so a later take pasted into the same box never goes out with the earlier send) (a 10-minute guard against a stuck take; it was 60 s, which a long LLM pass outlasted). A press may still hand a take off while it waits its turn, not once it pastes. A cancel during a handed-off take's paste leaves the clipboard to it. App Nap stays off while any handed-off take finishes.
- The island draws a pyramid (`NotchIslandActivityStack`, `NotchIslandLayerStackView`): the newest thing on top, older takes behind it newest first, a recording meeting always deepest, at most three layers; each 10 pt narrower per side and 9 pt lower, a shade lighter, with only a small animation in its peek (meeting: red dot and levels; processing: dots and a wave; queued: a ring); shadows by height above the bottom layer (none at the bottom). With no take of its own the island shows the newest finishing one as Writing. The layers tuck in while a drop-down is open. The controller pushes the takes and their stage (processing, queued, pasting) with `NotchIslandController.updateBackgroundTakes`.
- The hover lists everything as cards, newest first, meeting last (`NotchIslandDrop.activities`, `NotchIslandDropView+Activities.swift`): the take being spoken (Cancel, Insert), a take being written or finishing behind (Don't paste), the meeting (Stop). An "esc" key marks the card Esc acts on.
- Meetings queue the same way (fork): Stop hands the recording to the transcription queue, so the next meeting can start and record while the earlier one is transcribed. While a meeting starts or records, the queue's work sits deepest behind it (`NotchIslandMeetingContent.withEarlierMeeting`, layer `transcribingMeetingLayer`), and its hover keeps the recording's own controls with an "Earlier meeting · Transcribing 42%" line under Stop (with a dictation on top, the cards list it last). Its whole percent is a live value (`earlierMeetingPercent`, `NotchIslandMeetingUpdate.liveValuesOnly`), updated in place, never a rebuild. Whether a meeting starts or records is read from the session itself: right after Stop a meeting's own job starts while the overlay still shows it recording. A meeting that is only being transcribed no longer holds back a call prompt (the prompt takes the wings, the meeting goes behind as that layer) or an earlier meeting's speaker review: both wait only while a meeting starts or records (`isStartingOrRecording`). The finished chime stays quiet while another meeting records.
- Esc acts on the newest take still spoken or finishing: the take on the island first (the overlay's monitor, as before), else, with none there, the newest take finishing behind it that isn't pasting or declined (`declineNewestHandedOffTakePaste`, its own monitor while such takes exist): it won't paste and is saved. Each Esc takes the next one back. Esc never reaches a meeting; only Stop on its card, the menu or the hotkey stops it. Declined sessions are `pasteDeclinedSessionIDs` and are checked again after a take's turn wait.
- Quit doesn't wait for handed-off takes; their stopped audio stays on disk and launch recovery saves them.
- The send behavior (`DictationSendSettings`, the app's own or Default) holds per take: a handed-off take keeps whether it started with the Dictate & send key (`startedWithDictateAndSendKey`) and its own send decision, anchor and paste target, so each take presses send, or asks to, in its own box. With After I confirm, every take gets its own prompt, asked in turn (`+SendConfirmation`).

## Rules

- **Keep the product surface.** Meeting detection's record / dismiss / remind flow and the speaker review stay reachable from the island.
- **Out of screen capture by default.** The island panel sets `sharingType = .none`, or `.readOnly` only when the user turned on `NotchIslandPreferences.visibleInScreenSharing`. `OverlayScreenSharePrivacyTests` checks both.
- **Never steal focus.** Panels are non-activating. The island takes the keyboard only while someone types a name in "Who spoke?" (`acceptsKeyForTyping`); when naming ends or the review leaves the screen, `NotchIslandController` hands the keyboard back to the app they were in without hiding the island or activating Transcripted. The panel may sit over the menu bar.
- **AppKit renderers, controller-owned state.** Controllers push `update(...)` into views; no SwiftUI hosting in the island.
- **AirPods.** Nothing here builds an `AVAudioEngine` or touches `inputNode`. The start click waits for recording on a headset (`DictationStartCuePolicy`); read `Sources/Speech/AGENTS.md` before changing start timing.
- **Source pins.** `DictationSessionController.swift` and `MeetingOverlayController.swift` are heavily pinned; run `python3 scripts/dev/check-source-pins.py --changed-only` before editing.
